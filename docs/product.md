# First Thought

## Product

A morning wake-up app that turns the first 10–15 minutes of the day into a guided emotional reset.

### Core idea

**Alarm → wake up → breathe → meditation → choose how to meet the day  → then the day begins**


Instead of waking up to a harsh alarm and immediately checking the phone, the user wakes gently and transitions directly into a short guided meditation.

> **What is the first thought you put into your mind every morning?**

---

## Product experience

### Before bed

The user chooses:

* Alarm time
* Meditation length: 10–15 minutes
* Morning intention/mood

Possible intentions:

* Calm
* Clear
* Confident
* Grounded
* Focused
* Energized
* Grateful
* Letting go

Then they simply go to sleep.

### Morning

**7:00 AM**
→ Gentle alarm

**7:01 AM**
→ Alarm ends
→ Ambient sound slowly begins

**7:02 AM**
→ Guided meditation starts

**7:02–7:15 AM**
→ Meditation

**7:15 AM**
→ Meditation ends
→ User starts the day

The experience should require almost no interaction.

---

## Emotional tone

The app should feel:

* Calm
* Intelligent
* Warm
* Human
* Grounded
* Simple
* Non-cheesy

Avoid forced positivity, excessive spirituality, and generic motivational language.

Instead of:

> “Today is going to be AMAZING! You can achieve anything!”

Prefer:

> “You don't need to figure everything out today.
> You don't need to know what will happen.
> For now, just notice that you're here.”

The voice should feel like a calm person sitting beside you.

---

## Sound direction

Avoid stereotypical meditation music.

Music should create **space**, rather than announce “this is meditation.”

Possible sounds:

* Very soft jing
* Single piano notes
* Subtle ambient tones
* Soft drum beats
* Gentle morning atmosphere
* Very subtle birds
* Rain
* Near-silence

The sound should feel like **morning light**.

### During meditation

Keep the screen extremely minimal.

Possible visual concept:

**dark → dawn → light**

The screen gradually becomes lighter as the meditation progresses, mirroring sunrise and waking up.

## First meditation

### Theme

**You don't have to figure everything out today.**

This is the first meditation to develop into a complete 10–15 minute script.

Opening concept:

> Good morning.
>
> Before you get up, give yourself a moment.
>
> You don't have to solve anything yet.
> You don't have to know how today will unfold.
>
> Just notice that you're awake.
>
> Take a slow breath in.
>
> And let it go.
>
> This is the first few minutes of your day.
> Nothing has happened yet.
>
> You get to decide what you want to bring into it.

The full script should be developed collaboratively and edited until it sounds natural and emotionally authentic.

## AI opportunities

AI could eventually personalize the meditation based on:

* Morning intention
* Previous mood
* User-written thoughts
* Sleep quality
* Preferred meditation length
* Voice preference
* Upcoming events

The AI should feel invisible. The experience should feel human rather than obviously AI-generated.

---

## Important product principle

The app should **not tell people how they should feel**.

It should help them intentionally choose how they want to approach the day.

Core philosophy:


> You don't know what today will bring.
> You don't need to. 
> You chose how you feel and what your intention is.

## Working principle for this project

Don't overbuild.

The first goal is not to create a meditation platform.

The first goal is to answer one question:

**“Does this app help me choose my intention for the day?”**
